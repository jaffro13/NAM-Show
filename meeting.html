Model of the Month

A friendly, low-key competition among club members - bring a model, get a vote, see who wins.

Model of the Month runs at every regular club meeting. It's simple: bring along a model you've finished (or are working on), grab a number for it at the table, and enter it in a couple of taps. Everyone at the meeting then votes for their favourite - 1st and 2nd picks - and the model with the most points takes out the month.

No pressure, no big categories to worry about - just a fun way to show off what you've been building and see everyone else's work too. Bring as many models as you like.
