Monthly Meeting

N.A.M meets once a month on the third Saturday of each month, which will sometimes change if it clashes with another major event or public holiday, so check back here for info or check the Events Page.

Next Meeting
Saturday 15 August
Location
Greenbrook Community House
40 MacFarlane Cres, Epping VIC

Get Directions →

Time
7:30pm - 9:30pm

Our club meetings are not all strictly business, it's an evening for catching up with members and other guests to share tips, tricks and show off recently completed builds, new purchases and/or works in progress.

Each month we hold a "Model of the Month" competition where the club votes for their favourite build presented by fellow members.

There is free tea and coffee available, along with a small store where snacks and drinks can be purchased.

Everyone is welcome, you don't have to be a member but if you like what you see, you're welcome to join.
