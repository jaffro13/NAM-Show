Swap & Sell

Buy, sell and swap kits and tools, and chat with fellow modellers - open to the public, everyone welcome.

Whether you're clearing out stash you'll never build, hunting for that one kit to finish a project, or just after a good chat with fellow modellers, the Swap & Sell is a relaxed afternoon for the whole hobby community - not just club members.

Grab a table and sell your own kits, tools, and reference material, or just come along to browse and see what turns up. Doors open early for vendors setting up, with the public welcome from 2:30pm.
