***Modified diecast can be any diecast model that has been modified in some way (paint, addons, etc). If possible, please provide a photo of the original model before the modifications, to display alongside your model.

Models may be entered in one class only. All models are eligible for Best in Show and People's Choice. Australian subjects are automatically eligible for the Simon Breust Memorial Trophy. Trophies are awarded for 1st place in each class, People's Choice, Best in Show, and the Simon Breust Memorial Trophy. 2nd and 3rd place in each class receive a certificate.
