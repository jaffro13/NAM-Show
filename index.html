About the Show

A Day for Makers and Modellers

The Modelling Competition and Swap & Sell is Northern Area Modellers' annual celebration of scale modelling - bring along your best builds, get judged across 24 competition classes, and see how you stack up against fellow modellers from across the region. But at its heart, this day is about the fun of it all - catching up with fellow modellers, swapping hints, tips and techniques, and enjoying a day among people who love the hobby as much as you do.

Judging will be N.N.L Style, judged by entrants. This year we are introducing a brand new electronic voting system to speed up the vote counting process and reduce delays. All spectators through the door can also vote for People's Choice. There'll be swap & sell tables from fellow hobbyists and local traders, plus a raffle running throughout the show.

Every dollar raised on the day - entry fees, raffle tickets, swap & sell table takings - goes directly to our chosen charity.

Pricing

Entry Fees & Visiting

Entering a model is priced per model, with a cap of $20, so there's no reason to hold your best builds back. Just visiting? Spectator entry is a flat fee, with a family pass available. There's no set limit to the number of children you can bring on a family pass.
