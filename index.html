About the Show

A Day for Makers and Modellers

The N.N.L Model Show is Northern Area Modellers' annual celebration of scale modelling - bring along your best builds, get judged across 24 competition classes, and see how you stack up against fellow modellers from across the region.

Alongside the competition, spectators get their own say too - everyone through the door can vote for their favourite model on the day. There'll be trade tables from local hobby suppliers and a raffle running throughout the show.

Every dollar raised on the day - entry fees, raffle tickets, trade table takings - goes directly to charity.

Getting Involved

How Entry Works

01

Enter Online

Fill in the entry form with your details and each model you're bringing - takes a few minutes.

02

Check In On The Day

Bring your models along and check in at the door - walk-in entries are welcome too.

03

On Display & Judged

Your models go on display for judging across the day, and for everyone to enjoy.

04

Voting & Awards

Entrants and spectators vote, judges award each class, and trophies are presented.

Entry Fees

Simple, Capped Pricing

Entry is priced per model, with the cost dropping the more you bring - and it's capped, so there's no reason to hold your best builds back.

First 5 models $3.00 each
Next 5 models (6th-10th) $1.00 each
11th model onward FREE
Junior / Intermediate (under 18) FREE
24 Classes

Something For Everyone

Aircraft, armour, cars, ships, sci-fi, figures, dioramas and more - plus a dedicated Junior/Intermediate class for builders under 18.

Vote & Win

People's Choice & Simon Breust Trophy

Entrants and spectators both get to vote for their favourite model on the day, plus a dedicated trophy for the best Australian-subject build.
