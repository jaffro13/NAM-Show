Northern Area Modellers is a club for scale modellers of all kinds - whether you build aircraft, armour, cars, ships, sci-fi, or anything else that comes in a box with instructions. We meet regularly to share builds, swap tips, and enjoy the hobby together.

Our biggest event of the year is our Modelling Competition, held annually with dozens of classes, spectator voting, and trophies across the board. We also run a Model Kit Swap & Sell a few times a year, and Model of the Month keeps things going in between at our regular meetings.

New to the Club?

Come along to a meeting, enter the next show, or just say hello - everyone's welcome.
