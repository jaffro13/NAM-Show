About the Show

A Day for Makers and Modellers

The N.N.L Modelling Competition & Swap Meet is Northern Area Modellers' annual celebration of scale modelling - bring along your best builds, get judged across 24 competition classes, and see how you stack up against fellow modellers from across the region.

Alongside the competition, spectators get their own say too - everyone through the door can vote for their favourite model on the day. There'll be swap & sell tables from fellow hobbyists and local traders, plus a raffle running throughout the show.

Every dollar raised on the day - entry fees, raffle tickets, swap & sell table takings - goes directly to Soldier On.

04

Entrants and spectators vote, judges award each class, and trophies are presented.

Pricing

Entry Fees & Visiting

Entering a model is priced per model, with the cost dropping the more you bring - and it's capped, so there's no reason to hold your best builds back. Just visiting? Spectator entry is a flat fee, with a family pass available.

24 Classes

Something For Everyone

Aircraft, armour, cars, ships, sci-fi, figures, dioramas and more - plus a dedicated Junior/Intermediate class for builders under 18. Models may be entered in one class only.

Vote & Win

Best in Show, People's Choice & Simon Breust Memorial Trophy

All models are eligible for Best in Show and People's Choice. Australian-subject builds are automatically eligible for the Simon Breust Memorial Trophy too.
